Between Minds Project exists to bridge the gap between complex psychological research and the lived reality of relationships, parenting, and healing.

## Shammi Bappert (M.A. Clinical Psychology, PhD Researcher)

I chose to pause my PhD research on the dyadic analysis of psychopathology and dark personality traits to bring "gatekept" psychological insights to the public. My mission is to translate clinical expertise into everyday healing.

## Alisa (Co-Founder)

I am dedicated to creating a safe, non-judgmental sanctuary where the "shadow" parts of our stories can be shared and healed. I ensure our community feels seen and supported.

## How we work together

Shammi leads the clinical framing, research translation, and long-form writing.
Alisa shapes the emotional experience, visual softness, and sense of safety around the work.

Together, we build a space that feels rigorous, compassionate, and usable.
