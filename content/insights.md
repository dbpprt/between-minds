Insights is where Between Minds Project slows down enough to think in full sentences. It is the writing space for the questions that do not fit neatly into a caption or a quick answer.

## What lives here

- reflections on relationship dynamics, manipulation, boundaries, and emotional patterns
- writing on conscious parenting and the early years of raising emotionally intelligent children
- self-awareness work shaped by emotional intelligence and CBT-informed thinking

## Why writing matters

Sometimes people do not need more noise. They need language. They need a frame that helps them understand what is happening inside them and between them. The writing here is built for exactly that.

## How it connects to the wider project

The insights page sits alongside Shammi's 1-to-1 sessions, workshops, courses, and the book *Puddles, Patience and Play*. The same approach runs through all of it: psychological depth, clear language, and work that respects real life.
