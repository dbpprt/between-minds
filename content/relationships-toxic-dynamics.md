This pathway is for people who feel disoriented inside a relationship and want language that is clinically grounded, emotionally clear, and practically useful.

## What this work focuses on

- Identifying invisible patterns before they become normalised
- Naming coercive, confusing, or manipulative behaviour without self-blame
- Rebuilding internal clarity after chronic relational stress
- Understanding dark personality traits without sensationalising them

## What you will find here

Between Minds Project approaches relationship dynamics with steadiness rather than panic. The goal is not to dramatise harm. It is to help you understand what is happening, regain orientation, and respond from a more grounded sense of self.

## Why this matters

When relational confusion goes unnamed, people often lose confidence in their own perception. This page exists to restore language, boundaries, and psychological perspective.
